We build where the grid ends.

Solar power for a panchayat, a classroom back online, an early warning before the elephants reach the fence.

Join the branch See the field work

Three projects, none of them on campus.

Light the Lives, 2019

A week living in Kuttampuzha, and two solar systems left behind.

Volunteers stayed in the panchayat, surveyed what the grid had never reached, then installed two working solar power systems.

Connect the Lives, 2021

A tribal colony school in Uriyampetty got its classes back online.

We built and fitted the communication module. A resident teacher, trained by the branch, still runs it today.

Wild Elephant Tracker

An early warning system watching the fringe where the forest meets the fields.

Movement at the treeline triggers a real time alert, so the village knows long before the herd arrives.

Enhance their Lives, upcoming

Sanitary pad incinerators and waste disposal for remote schools.

The next one on the list. Same approach as the last three: go there, build it, leave it working.
