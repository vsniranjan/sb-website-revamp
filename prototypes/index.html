IEEE MACE SB, three redesign directions

  1. A. The Index

    Light, type-led, near-zero decoration. Everything the branch does presented as an index. Body copy hides behind interaction so fifty items read as fifty lines.

  2. B. Fieldwork

    Dark, photo-led. Leads with the solar install, the school connectivity module, the elephant tracker. Photography carries the page, type stays sparse.

  3. C. Instrument

    Light, data-led. Engineering register built from real numbers instead of drawn gauges. 1988, 38 years, 300+ members, 9 chapters.
